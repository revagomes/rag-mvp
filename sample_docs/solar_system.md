# The Solar System

The Solar System consists of the Sun and everything gravitationally bound to it.
This includes eight planets, their moons, dwarf planets, and countless smaller
bodies such as asteroids and comets.

## The Sun

The Sun is a G-type main-sequence star that contains about 99.8% of the total
mass of the Solar System. Its energy, produced by nuclear fusion of hydrogen
into helium, powers almost all life on Earth.

## The Planets

The four inner planets — Mercury, Venus, Earth, and Mars — are terrestrial
planets with solid rocky surfaces. The four outer planets — Jupiter, Saturn,
Uranus, and Neptune — are gas and ice giants.

Jupiter is the largest planet in the Solar System, with a mass more than twice
that of all the other planets combined. Saturn is famous for its prominent ring
system, made mostly of ice particles with a smaller amount of rocky debris.

## Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only known place in the universe
where life exists. About 71% of its surface is covered by water. Earth has one
natural satellite, the Moon, which stabilizes the planet's axial tilt and drives
the ocean tides.
